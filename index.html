Did You Know?

Mind-Blowing Facts

🌊

Swimming tigers

Tigers are excellent swimmers and genuinely enjoy water — unlike most cats. They often cool off in rivers and ponds.

🖤

Skin like stripes

A tiger's stripes aren't just fur — the skin underneath is striped too. Every pattern is unique to the individual.

📢

Loudest cat

A lion's roar can carry up to 8 kilometres across open savanna. A tiger's can be heard about 3 km away.

👑

Pride dynamics

Lionesses do most of the hunting, while males patrol and defend the pride's territory from rivals.

🌙

Night hunters

Tigers are primarily nocturnal. Their night vision is roughly six times more sensitive than a human's.

⚡

Short bursts

Both cats are sprinters, not marathon runners. Lions and tigers rely on explosive speed over very short distances.
